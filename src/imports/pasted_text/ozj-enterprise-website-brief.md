Design and build a complete, responsive, production-quality corporate website for OZJ Enterprise Limited, an African energy, procurement and technical services company.

The website should be designed as a premium B2B oil, gas, energy and industrial supply company website. It must look credible enough for institutional clients, oil and gas companies, banks, manufacturers, construction companies, marine operators, offshore operators and critical infrastructure organisations.

IMPORTANT:
Do not make this look like a generic oil and gas template.
Do not use excessive gradients, glowing effects, cheesy oil-drop graphics, excessive rounded cards, or generic startup aesthetics.

The visual standard should feel comparable to premium international energy and commodity companies such as Trafigura, Vitol and leading engineering and energy companies, while retaining a strong African identity.
